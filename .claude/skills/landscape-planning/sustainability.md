# Sustainability and Modern Standards

## The SITES Rating System (Sustainable SITES Initiative)

Administered by Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI) — the landscape equivalent of LEED for buildings.

Rating Categories:
- Site Context — Understanding surrounding context, ecosystem services
- Pre-Design Assessment and Planning — Site inventory, stakeholder engagement, performance goals
- Water — Stormwater management, water conservation, water quality
- Soil and Vegetation — Soil health, native plants, invasive species management, urban heat reduction
- Materials — Sustainable sourcing, recycled content, regional materials, reducing waste
- Human Health and Well-Being — Outdoor activity, mental restoration, accessibility, safety
- Construction — Minimizing disturbance, controlling erosion, protecting existing features
- Operations and Maintenance — Adaptive management, reduced inputs, organic practices
- Education and Performance Monitoring — Public engagement, long-term performance tracking
- Innovation — Exemplary performance beyond standard credits

Certification Levels (out of 200 points):
- Certified — 70 points
- Silver — 85 points
- Gold — 100 points
- Platinum — 135 points

## Sustainable Design Strategies

- Green Infrastructure — Rain gardens, bioswales, green roofs, permeable paving, constructed wetlands
- Low-Impact Development (LID) — Manage stormwater at the source; mimic predevelopment hydrology
- Xeriscaping — Water-efficient landscaping using drought-tolerant and native plants
- Urban Forestry — Strategic tree planting for shade, air quality, carbon sequestration, stormwater interception
- Soil Restoration — De-compact, amend, and protect soils as living systems
- Adaptive Reuse — Repurpose existing materials, structures, infrastructure
- Dark Sky Design — Minimize light pollution through shielded, low-intensity, warm-toned lighting
